How Civixo Works

Reporting a local problem takes only a few simple steps.

1
📝

Report

Describe the public problem you noticed in your local area.

2
📍

Add Location

Add the problem location using GPS, manually, or through the map.

3
🔒

Submit Anonymously

Submit your complaint without publicly revealing your identity.

4
📊

Track

Keep your complaint ID and check the status of your submitted issue.

Garbage & Waste

💡

Street Lights

💧

🚰

Drainage & Sewage

Report blocked drains, sewage overflow and drainage issues.

⚡

Electricity

Report public electricity-related problems in your area.

🚦

Traffic Problems

Report traffic-related problems, unsafe signals or road conditions.

📢

Other Issues
